Our Services

Eleven ways we support older adults and their families, from everyday personal care to relaxing wellness therapies.

Request Care 059 971 2662

Care & support

Everyday care for older adults

Practical, dignified help that lets older adults stay comfortable, safe and independent.

Personal Care Giving

Respectful, one-to-one help with the activities of daily living, always at the client's own pace.

  • Bathing, dressing and grooming
  • Mobility support and safe movement
  • Meal preparation and help with eating
  • Medication reminders and companionship

Respite / Short-Term Caregiving

Dependable, temporary care that gives family carers time to rest, travel or attend to other needs, knowing their loved one is in safe hands.

  • Cover for a few days or several weeks
  • Support during recovery after hospital
  • Keeps the client's usual routines

Wound Care Assistance

Careful, hygienic help with caring for wounds and skin, following the guidance of the client's doctor or nurse.

  • Help with dressing changes
  • Keeping wounds clean and protected
  • Watching for changes and reporting concerns

Aged Day Care

A safe, welcoming daytime setting where older adults can enjoy company, activities and care while family members are at work.

  • Supervision and personal care
  • Social time and gentle activities
  • Home in the evening with family

Wellbeing & guidance

Support for the mind, heart and family

Ageing brings change. We support older adults and the families who love them.

Mental & Emotional Support

Warm companionship, conversation and encouragement that help ease loneliness, worry and low mood, and help clients feel heard and valued.

Counselling

A safe, confidential space for older adults and their families to talk through difficult changes, loss and the challenges of caring for a loved one.

Consultancy

Professional advice on planning and arranging the right care for an older family member, and on setting up good aged-care practices.

Training

Practical training in caring for older adults safely and respectfully, for family carers and caregivers who want to build their skills.

Relax & restore

Wellness therapies

Soothing therapies that help the body relax and the mind unwind.

Reflexology

Gentle pressure applied to points on the feet and hands to promote relaxation and a sense of wellbeing.

Massage

Relaxing massage that helps ease stiffness and muscle tension, and supports comfort and better rest.

Sauna

Soothing warmth that relaxes the muscles and helps clients unwind, offered with care and supervision.

How it works

  3. Agree a care plan

    We provide consistent care and keep the family informed.
