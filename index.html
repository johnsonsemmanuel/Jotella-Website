FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who do you care for?

We care for older adults and anyone who needs support with daily living, whether that's help with personal care, mobility, meals, medication reminders or simply companionship.

How do we get started?

Call or WhatsApp us on 059 971 2662, or fill in our contact form. We'll talk with you about your loved one's needs and preferences and agree a care plan that suits your family.

Do you offer short-term or temporary care?

Yes. Our respite and short-term caregiving gives family carers a break, whether for a few days, while travelling or during recovery after a hospital stay.

What is aged day care?

Aged day care is a safe, friendly setting where older adults can spend the day with company and supervision, then return home in the evening. It's ideal for families who work during the day.

Do you work with family members and doctors?

Yes. We work closely with families and, where appropriate, with healthcare professionals so that every client receives consistent, dependable care.

Where are you located?
