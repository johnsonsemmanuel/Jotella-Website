About Jotella

Compassionate, respectful and professional care for older adults, because every person deserves to age with dignity, comfort and companionship.

Our mottoCompassion is our calling, care is our commitment.

Who we are

Quality care that goes beyond physical needs

At Jotella Aged Home Care Limited, we are committed to providing compassionate, respectful and professional care for older adults and individuals who require support in their daily lives.

We understand that every person is unique, and we believe that quality care goes beyond meeting physical needs. Our approach focuses on promoting dignity, comfort, companionship, independence and overall wellbeing.

Our trained caregivers provide personalised support tailored to each client's needs and preferences. From personal care and companionship to help with mobility, meals, medication reminders and daily activities, we strive to make every client feel safe, valued and cared for.

We also understand how important it is for families to have peace of mind. We work closely with families and, where appropriate, healthcare professionals so that clients receive consistent, dependable care.

What drives us

Our vision & mission

Our Vision

To become a trusted leader in dignified, compassionate and professionally regulated aged home care in Ghana.

Our Mission

Providing safe, respectful, person-centred and culturally responsive care services that enhance the quality of life, independence, wellbeing and protection of elderly persons and their families.

Our values

Compassion

Dignity & Respect

Safety & Protection

Person-Centred Care

Cultural Responsiveness

Independence

Meet the team

The caring people behind Jotella

Dedicated professionals committed to the comfort, dignity, safety and wellbeing of every client.

Executive Director

Agnes Oppong-Baah

Agnes is a Ghanaian nurse-administrator with over 33 years of experience in clinical nursing and healthcare regulation. Her expertise includes nursing regulation, examinations, accreditation, policy development, healthcare administration and institutional leadership.

As Executive Director of Jotella Aged Home Care Limited, she oversees operations and the quality of care given to every client. The love and compassion she shows every day is reflected in all the work she does.

Administrator

Anita Afi Senah

Anita is a dedicated administrator committed to promoting compassionate, professional and dignified care for older adults. She supports administration, staff development and quality care standards, with a strong focus on empathy, respect and client wellbeing.
